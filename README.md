## About me

I'm a Computer Science student at MIT ADT University, Pune (graduating 2027), and an AI systems engineer. I build AI products end to end, from the model call to the interface someone actually uses, and I write about how they work in plain English.

- 🎙️ **Now:** AI Voice Agents & Automation Engineer at **Quensulting AI** (May 2026 – present). I build Retell and Vapi voice agents and connect calls to CRMs, backends and WhatsApp through n8n.
- 🧠 **Before:** GenAI Intern at **Persistent Systems** (Sep 2025 – Apr 2026): agentic RAG workflows, LLM evaluation, and a digital-twin agent that drafts email replies in your own writing style.
- ✍️ **Writing:** a technical blog on GenAI, RAG, MCP, agents and local AI, with architecture diagrams and real benchmarks: [ayushchougula.in/blog](https://ayushchougula.in/blog)
- 🔎 **Curious about:** retrieval that doesn't hallucinate, running models locally on Apple Silicon, and AI-assisted documentation
- 📫 **Open to:** AI engineering and content engineering internships, and open-source collaboration
